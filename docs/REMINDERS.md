# Local reminders and occurrence rules

MetricMe uses inexact AlarmManager `setAndAllowWhileIdle` alarms for user-chosen local wall-clock times. WorkManager maintains durable one-time delivery fallbacks and a six-hour reconciliation job. Reconciliation also runs at app start/resume, after edits, and after boot, own-package replacement, clock or timezone changes. There is no exact-alarm permission or exact-delivery promise. Android restrictions, Doze, OEM battery limits, reboot delays and force-stop can delay or prevent delivery; after force-stop, open the app again.

Notifications are opt-in. Android 13+ requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS only from the Enable reminders action. Denial does not block logging or occurrence actions. Names, instructions and dose text are absent from both normal notification text and lock-screen previews. Android notification settings remain accessible from the routine screen.

Each schedule has immutable versions, one local time per schedule and selected weekdays. Edits close old versions and cancel future pending occurrences while keeping past and confirmed history. Version and occurrence snapshots retain the original label/instructions. Archiving changes the app's tracking only; it never instructs a user to stop treatment. PRN records are separate taken events with no scheduled adherence denominator. A course's last day is inclusive; its end instant is the next local midnight.

Occurrences store a scheduled instant and applicable offset; versions retain the IANA zone. Device-zone changes close the current versions, preserve historical offsets and generate future versions at the same wall time in the new zone. Future start/end calendar dates are kept local. DST gaps shift reminder time forward; overlaps use the earlier offset once. User-entered log timestamps instead reject nonexistent times and require an explicit offset for repeated times.

Occurrence UUIDs derive from version + scheduled instant + offset. Unique database constraints prevent duplicate planning. Alarm PendingIntent identity uses the occurrence UUID; WorkManager names include occurrence and due instant. At most 64 upcoming/recent alerts are scheduled at a time, and reconciliation refills the window. This avoids Android's per-app alarm limit. A recorded daily step total supersedes individual walk step subtotals; this has no relationship to reminders.

Delivery rechecks enabled settings, active schedule/course, occurrence status and current due instant, then atomically claims that due instant. An alert more than two hours late is not delivered. A crash or denied permission never changes a dose to taken, skipped or missed. Delivery is best effort; a crash after claiming may suppress an alert, while the occurrence stays not recorded.

Taken/Done, Skip and medication Snooze actions use transactional operation receipts. Terminal states cannot be overwritten by competing notification actions; the user can explicitly undo a status in the app. A snooze changes due time without creating a new occurrence. It is ten minutes after now or the original due time, whichever is later. Old notification actions with a superseded due instant, disabled reminders or an ended schedule are discarded. Repeated actions with the same key have no extra effect. No action recommends dose changes or doubling up.

App reminders use private content and no health-data network calls. WorkManager may merge normal Android infrastructure permissions such as wake lock and network-state access; these do not authorize health-data upload. Camera, microphone, photo AI, account authentication and remote services belong to Stage 3.

References verified during Stage 2: [Android alarm guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms), [notification permission](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications/notification-permission), and [WorkManager releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/work). WorkManager is pinned to 2.12.0 in the version catalog.
